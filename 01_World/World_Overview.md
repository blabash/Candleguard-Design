# World Overview

Candleguard takes place beneath the surface in sprawling kobold tunnel kingdoms. Kobold society is built around digging, scavenging, hauling, beast handling, candle ritual, and fighting over valuable underground materials called Shinies.

The world should feel like a living industrial ecosystem: mines, carts, fungal networks, rat crews, mole cavalry, crystal veins, half-collapsed ruins, candle shrines, and unstable tunnel fronts.

## Kobolds

Candleguard kobolds are not helpless comic relief. They are small, egotistical, reckless, funny, and often ridiculous, but they are also excellent engineers, terrifying tunnel fighters, brilliant scavengers, and capable beast tamers.

Their culture rewards confidence, cleverness, survival instinct, and the ability to turn tools into weapons.

## Candles

Candles are a major cultural symbol because the Deep is dangerous. A flame does more than light the way. It warns. It reacts. It tells a trained kobold that something is wrong before the tunnel itself does.

Common saying:

> Never let your candle die.

Possible cultural answer to why kobolds wear candles:

> Because the Deep watches.

## Shinies

Shinies are the crude kobold term for valuable underground materials. They may include crystals, ores, luminous fragments, relic pieces, or other resources that kobolds mine, hoard, trade, and fight over.

The exact gameplay use of Shinies is still open, but they are already important as world language and motivation.

## Hidden Mycelium

A hidden mycelial network subtly empowers underground life and kobold capability. Most kobolds do not understand it and may not know it exists.

The network should feel like a background force rather than an explained magic system at first. It can connect tunnels, affect creatures, alter resource growth, and hint that the underground world is more alive than kobolds realize.

## The Deep and the Mouth Below

The Deep is not merely deeper terrain. It is the region where the rules begin to bend.

The Mouth Below is a major mythic or cosmic threat associated with the deepest places. It does not need to be fully explained early. Its presence can be suggested through candle behavior, tunnel anomalies, corrupted zones, and kobold superstition.

The Mouth Below does not need to fear swords, magic, or crystals. It should make candles burn strangely.
