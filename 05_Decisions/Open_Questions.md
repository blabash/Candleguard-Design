# Open Questions

This file holds unresolved decisions and design prompts.

## World

- What are the starting factions or teams called?
- Are factions permanent societies, temporary warbands, mining companies, clans, candle orders, or expedition crews?
- What exactly are Shinies made of?
- Are Shinies mundane resources, magical materials, mycelial byproducts, relic fragments, or all of the above?
- What does a candle detect in precise world terms?
- Who first discovered candle warning behavior?
- What happens culturally when a kobold lets their candle die?
- How much do kobolds understand about the hidden mycelial network?
- Is the Mouth Below a creature, place, god, tunnel system, event, or myth?

## Gameplay

- What are Shinies mechanically used for during a match?
- Does the game have items, upgrades, temporary lane purchases, or champion-specific improvements?
- Are lane towers machines, creatures, shrine-structures, faction devices, or living defenses?
- What is the final win condition called in-world?
- Should resurrection have a lore explanation or stay as pure match logic?
- Can players dig, alter, or temporarily reroute parts of the lane?
- How many objectives can a single-lane map support before it stops feeling focused?

## Champions

- What is the first playable champion prototype?
- Should champion names be personal names, class titles, or both?
- Which champion best sells the identity of Candleguard in a trailer?
- How much comedy should be in champion voice and animation?

## Art

- What is the exact default kobold anatomy language?
- How do we keep candle imagery distinct from Warcraft?
- Which candle placements are signature Candleguard motifs?
- What is the strongest visual rule for Shinies?
- How exaggerated can proportions become before gameplay readability suffers?
