# Game Vision

Candleguard is a competitive hero game about kobold champions, tunnel warfare, improvised engineering, and the dangerous culture that forms around surviving underground.

The game should feel immediately readable and fun: a team of bizarre little specialists pushes down a contested lane, fights over resources, escorts waves of worker-creatures, and cracks open enemy defenses through clever aggression. Underneath that, the setting has a stranger heartbeat: candles react to threats, Shinies are more than valuables, and the Deep is not just empty darkness.

## Core Promise

Win fights, push the lane, exploit the underground, and protect your flame.

## Player Fantasy

The player is not a generic soldier. They are a named kobold champion: a reckless specialist from a culture of miners, scavengers, beast tamers, tunnel engineers, and flame-bearers.

Each champion should feel like they had a real job before the match started. A champion might be an ember binder, mole rider, rat alchemist, rune duelist, crystal warden, or bat caller. Their combat kit should grow naturally from their role in kobold society.

## Match Shape

The default mode is a single-lane PvP push supporting 1v1, 2v2, and 3v3. The lane represents a contested underground front where two kobold forces collide while expanding, mining, scavenging, and weaponizing the tunnel around them.

## Long-Term North Star

Candleguard should become a world where players remember the creatures, slang, rituals, and weird battlefield logic as much as they remember the abilities.
