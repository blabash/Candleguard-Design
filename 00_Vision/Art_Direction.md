# Art Direction

Candleguard's visual style combines cartoony proportions with more grounded rendering.

Characters should have exaggerated bodies, large snouts, oversized ears, big hands, chunky tools, readable weapons, and dramatic posture. Materials can be rendered with believable texture and lighting: wax, soot, leather, crystal, tarnished metal, damp stone, fungal matter, bone, rope, and scavenged machinery.

## Current Preferred Character Direction

Use realistic material treatment over deliberately stylized proportions.

The latest preferred kobold reference direction is:

- cartoony but threatening proportions
- saturated colors
- strong silhouettes for gameplay readability
- large expressive faces and hands
- compact bodies with heavy gear
- heroic poses that are trying hard to look intimidating
- enough charm that the character remains memorable rather than generic

## Candle Motif

Candles are recurring world language. Avoid making every kobold the same single-candle-on-head silhouette.

Use variation:

- helmet lanterns
- candle crowns
- bone candle cages
- backpack candle rigs
- shoulder candles
- saddle candles
- hanging wick charms
- wagon shrine-hooks
- wax-sealed tools and armor
- flame-reactive warning devices

When the Deep or Mouth Below is near, candle behavior can become wrong:

- flame burns blue
- flame bends sideways
- flame burns black
- flame stops flickering
- wax runs upward
- light becomes too sharp or too dim

## Palette

The world should not be muddy. Use saturated color and high material contrast, especially for:

- flame and wax
- crystals and Shinies
- fungal glow
- team readability
- champion silhouettes
- danger states

Underground does not mean visually dull. Darkness is useful when it makes flame, color, and movement matter.

## Creep and Creature Direction

Creeps should be workers before soldiers. The best minions look like they belong in an underground economy and were only recently pointed toward the enemy lane.

Rat scavengers and mole bruisers currently use the candle motif. Their candles should feel like field equipment and faction culture, not a copied visual joke.
