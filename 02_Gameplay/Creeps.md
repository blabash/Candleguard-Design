# Creeps

Creeps in Candleguard are not generic fantasy minions. They are workers, beasts, carts, tools, constructs, scavengers, and tunnel-life pressed into battle.

The guiding question:

> What job did this thing perform five minutes before the kobolds decided to send it into battle?

## Current Creep Direction

Creeps should look useful before they look military. Their battle role should grow from their original job.

Examples:

- Rat Scavenger: a nimble collector trained to grab scraps, Shinies, and loose supplies; in battle it harasses, scouts, or steals.
- Mole Bruiser: a hauling or digging beast repurposed as a front-line pusher; in battle it absorbs damage and shoves through defenses.
- Drillcart: a mining vehicle redirected down-lane; in battle it threatens structures or creates temporary breaches.
- Rubblehead: a load-bearing worker creature or animated debris-hauler; in battle it clogs the lane and soaks hits.
- Shinyback: a resource-carrying creature that creates risk/reward when killed or escorted.

## Candle Integration

Recent direction adds candles to the Rat Scavenger and Mole Bruiser. Keep this motif, but vary the execution.

Rat Scavenger possibilities:

- small candle strapped to a scavenger cap
- wick charm dangling from a wire frame
- wax-dripped pack lantern
- candle attached to a little salvage pole

Mole Bruiser possibilities:

- heavy mining candle cage on brow armor
- saddle shrine with multiple candles
- cracked helmet lantern
- wax-streaked harness used by handlers

## Gameplay Readability

Each creep needs a clear silhouette and role:

- fragile harasser
- durable blocker
- structure threat
- resource carrier
- ranged nuisance
- support worker

Candles can help readability by indicating team, role, warning state, or empowered state.
